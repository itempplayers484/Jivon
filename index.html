Privacy Policy for Jivon

Application: Jivon Package Name: com.jivon.health Effective Date: September 25, 2026

🔒 100% Local-First Architecture

Jivon does not own, operate, or connect to remote user databases or cloud servers. All personal medical data, health records, notes, cycle tracking, and workout trails are stored solely on your physical device hardware.

🔒

Zero Cloud Storage

Your health data is stored strictly in your device's sandboxed filesystem with 256-Bit AES encryption.

🚫

Zero Telemetry

No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, crash loggers, trackers, or advertising SDKs.

🧠

On-Device AI & OCR

Clinical AI and OCR run entirely on your phone's processor. No queries or photos are sent to cloud AI servers.

📱

Hardware Biometrics

Vault locking uses Android BiometricPrompt hardware enclave; biometric data never leaves your device chip.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike conventional health applications, Jivon operates without a remote server backend:

  • No Account Creation: You do not need an email, phone number, password, or cloud account to use Jivon.
  • No Health Data Uploads: We never transmit, sync, host, or sell your health records, lab reports, doctor appointments, medications, cycle tracking, or GPS routes.
  • No Cloud AI Queries: Jivon's Clinical AI Assistant is powered by an offline, on-device heuristics engine. Queries never leave your phone.
  • No Commercial Analytics: We do not track user behavior or app screen views.

2. On-Device Data Storage & Hardware Permissions

Jivon accesses specific device features solely to provide local functionality:

  • Medical Vault & Document Storage: Documents, prescriptions, and lab values are stored in private local app storage, secured via on-device 256-Bit AES hardware encryption.
  • Camera & Media Files: Used strictly upon your explicit action to photograph prescriptions, lab reports, or scan food barcodes. Text recognition (OCR) is performed 100% on-device via Google ML Kit neural models.
  • Location Data (GPS): Used only during active, user-initiated outdoor workouts to calculate distance, speed, and draw route trails on a custom vector canvas. Location data is never broadcast or shared with mapping tile servers.
  • Physical Activity (Step Counter): Reads the Android hardware step sensor (Sensor.TYPE_STEP_COUNTER) locally to record daily steps.
  • Biometric Sensors: Uses Android's native BiometricPrompt to unlock your private vault. Your fingerprint or facial scan data is handled exclusively by your device's secure hardware enclave; Jivon never accesses biometric geometry.

3. Third-Party Service Disclosure: Open Food Facts

The only external network communication occurs when scanning a food barcode in the Diet tab:

  • Third-Party Service: Open Food Facts API (world.openfoodfacts.org).
  • When Activated: Only if a scanned barcode is not already found in Jivon’s built-in offline food library.
  • Data Sent: Only the numeric barcode digits (e.g., 8901030383792) via secure HTTPS.
  • Privacy Protection: No names, medical records, patient data, device identifiers, or GPS coordinates are ever transmitted. If the device is offline or in airplane mode, the lookup gracefully times out and allows manual input.

4. Optional Android Profile Backup

Jivon supports Android's standard OS-level backup (BackupManager), which is disabled by default for privacy:

  • If enabled manually in Settings, an encrypted snapshot of your profile is stored in your personal Google Drive account managed by Android OS.
  • The developers of Jivon do not have access to your Google account or backup files.
  • When disabled, local backup snapshots are permanently purged from your device.

5. User Data Sovereignty & Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your device, you maintain complete control:

  • You can view, edit, or delete any record, document, medication, or workout directly within the app.
  • Uninstalling Jivon or clearing the app data in Android Settings instantly and permanently erases all stored data from the device.
